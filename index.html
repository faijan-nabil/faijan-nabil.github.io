Short bio

I am an Assistant Professor in the Economics Discipline at Khulna University, Bangladesh. My research applies econometric methods to health, environmental and agricultural questions, including maternal healthcare, household and farmer responses to climate risk, and the economic cost of disease. I hold an MSS and a BSS (Hons) in Economics from Khulna University, both with distinction, and I have worked on research projects with FAO, UNICEF, the World Bank, CPD and SANEM.
